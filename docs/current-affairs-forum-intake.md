## Verification steps before anything is published

1. Open the category archive and page through it. The site's own JSON feed lists posts with author fields:
   `https://public-api.wordpress.com/rest/v1.1/sites/currentaffairsforum.wordpress.com/posts/?number=100`
2. Record, per post: byline exactly as printed, publication date, title, permalink.
3. Ask Professor al-Ahsan to confirm the list of his own pieces and to flag anything he does not want republished or linked.
4. Cross-check each confirmed piece against `/writing` and `data/core-works.json` for duplicates and reprints. A reprint is noted on the existing entry rather than listed twice.
5. Add confirmed pieces with the same labeling discipline used elsewhere: type tag, year, venue, link.
6. Capture an archival snapshot (Wayback Machine save) for each confirmed piece. A free WordPress.com blog can disappear without notice; the snapshot URL is the durable citation.

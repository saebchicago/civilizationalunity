Five conversations

Questions for a room

These summaries are concise guides into the published record. The scholarship linked elsewhere on the site remains the fuller source.

  • Dialogue or Clash: What the Record Actually Shows

    A response to clash-of-civilizations arguments built from the history of Muslim and European contact and al-Ahsan’s published work on civilizational dialogue, conflict and perception.

    Inquire about this topic →

  • Rise and Fall: How Civilizations Come Apart

    A conversation around human dignity, cooperation, governance and civilizational progress or decline, read against the historical record rather than as a deterministic formula.

    Inquire about this topic →

  • Human Dignity Before Law: Reading the Cairo Declaration

    Grounded in his study in the Journal of Law and Religion and his good-governance work with Stephen B. Young, this topic examines dignity, rights instruments and political institutions.

    Inquire about this topic →

  • The OIC at Fifty and After

    A long institutional view of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, drawing on scholarship that begins with his 1988 monograph and continues through later work on conflict resolution and Muslim-world diplomacy.

    Inquire about this topic →

  • Eurocentrism and the Method of History

    For history departments and graduate seminars: how historical vantage point shapes evidence, agency and the stories civilizations tell about one another.

    Inquire about this topic →

Bios

Three lengths

Short · about 40 words

Medium · about 100 words

Abdullah al-Ahsan is a historian of civilizations whose work examines relationships among human dignity, cooperation, governance and civilizational decline. He holds a PhD in History from the University of Michigan and an MA in Islamic Studies from McGill. He taught for nearly thirty years at the International Islamic University Malaysia, where he served as Deputy Dean of ISTAC and edited the journal Intellectual Discourse, and later held a chair in Comparative Civilization at Istanbul Şehir University. He is the author or editor of five books and a Fellow of the Caux Round Table.

Long · about 190 words

Abdullah al-Ahsan is a historian of civilizations. Across five books and more than fifty publications listed in his supplied CV, a recurring concern is the relationship among human dignity, cooperation, governance and civilizational change. He holds a PhD in History from the University of Michigan, an MA in Islamic Studies from McGill, and an MS in History from Quaid-i-Azam University. He taught at the International Islamic University Malaysia from 1990 to 2017, serving as Professor and Deputy Dean at ISTAC, Director of IIUM Press, and editor of the journal Intellectual Discourse, and was Professor of Comparative Civilization at Istanbul Şehir University from 2018 to 2020. His books include Ummah or Nation (1992), translated into Turkish and Bosnian, and Qur'anic Guidance for Good Governance (Palgrave Macmillan, 2017), co-edited with Stephen B. Young. He is a Fellow of the Caux Round Table and a former Vice President of the International Movement for a Just World. His recent public writing includes work published by Informed Comment, IslamiCity and Pegasus; dated links appear on the Writing page.

Getting it right in print

Name, spelling and citation

He publishes and signs as Abdullah al-Ahsan. Lower case al, hyphenated, capital A on Ahsan. Library catalogues and older citations also carry Abdullah Ahsan and Abdullahil Ahsan, which refer to the same person.

On first reference, Professor Abdullah al-Ahsan, a historian of civilizations is accurate and sufficient. If an institutional affiliation is needed, use Fellow of the Caux Round Table for Moral Capitalism. Do not describe him as a current officer of the International Movement for a Just World; that role is former.

For attribution

Short bio

Abdullah al-Ahsan is a historian of civilizations. He holds a PhD in History from the University of Michigan, held academic teaching and research appointments in Malaysia, Turkey, Pakistan and the United States, is the author or editor of five books, and is a Fellow of the Caux Round Table for Moral Capitalism.

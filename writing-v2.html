The published record

Writing

Five books and a wider record of articles, chapters and public essays trace a long inquiry into civilizational identity, institutions, governance, historical method and encounters between Muslim and Western worlds.

This is a selected, source-linked record rather than a claim of completeness. Publication types remain visible so readers can distinguish books and scholarly publications from public commentary.

Begin with five works

A route through the intellectual arc

Not a ranking. This is one compact path from institutions and identity to civilizational dialogue, historical method and governance.

  1. 01
    OIC: Introduction to an Islamic Political Institution (1988)Begin with the institutional question: a sustained early study of a modern Muslim-world political organization.
  2. 02
    Ummah or Nation? Identity Crisis in Contemporary Muslim Society (1992)Move from institutions to belonging and the tension between civilizational community and the modern nation-state.
  3. 03
    “Dialogue of Civilizations: Islamic and Western Perceptions” (2011)Enter the long-running inquiry into dialogue, conflict and the historical record of Muslim–Western encounter.
  4. 04
    “Problems of Eurocentric Views of History” (2013)Turn the lens toward historical method itself: how vantage point shapes the record.
  5. 05
    Qur’anic Guidance for Good Governance (2017)Return to institutions through the moral question of governance, co-edited with Stephen B. Young.

Trust is part of the scholarship

Editorial standard & accessibility

Civilizational Unity is designed as a public knowledge resource around Abdullah al-Ahsan’s work, not as an advertising page. Its standard is simple: make the scholarship easier to find, learn and reuse without blurring the line between the professor’s own words, the published record and editorial interpretation.

Source hierarchy

What counts as evidence here

1 · Direct wording

When Professor al-Ahsan supplies or corrects wording, the site treats that wording as primary for the site and labels it when useful.

2 · Scholarly record

Publication titles, dates, appointments and credentials are grounded in his supplied CV, publisher records, institutional pages, or authority identifiers.

3 · External reception

Reviews and third-party statements are quoted sparingly, attributed, and linked to the source when available.

4 · Editorial synthesis

Framework explanations, talk summaries and teaching prompts may synthesize the record. They are labeled as editorial and should not be cited as Professor al-Ahsan’s own wording.

No-hallucination rule

Polish never outranks provenance

A persuasive sentence is not kept merely because it sounds good. If a biographical claim, historical assertion, availability promise, numerical superlative or quotation cannot be traced to a reliable source, it is removed, softened, or explicitly labeled as editorial interpretation.

This is an editorial policy for this site, not a claim that every external source is infallible. Corrections should improve both the visible text and the underlying source trail.

Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA

The site is designed toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA rather than claiming formal certification. It uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, large controls, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, strong text contrast, and no autoplaying media.

Known limitation: embedded videos are hosted by third-party source channels. Caption and transcript availability and accuracy depend on those channels unless a separately verified transcript is linked here.

Search & AI discoverability

Optimize the record, not the hype

Important claims are kept in visible text, pages have descriptive titles and canonical URLs, internal links use descriptive language, and structured data identifies the scholar and site without adding claims that are not visible to readers. A small llms.txt file is provided as a convenience to systems that choose to use it; it is not presented as a ranking requirement.

Corrections & preservation

Designed to remain useful

The site intentionally uses static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build dependency for ordinary reading. Source links and authority identifiers are preferred over copied publication files. To flag a correction, source question, accessibility barrier or broken link, use the contact form and choose “Something else.”
